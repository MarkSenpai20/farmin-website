The Core Challenge

Why Rice Farmers Need FarmIn

Rice farming remains the economic backbone of Ilocos Norte, yet smallholders face severe market disadvantages:

  • Price Asymmetry: Farmers sell upon harvest due to financial urgency, often unaware of impending seasonal price surges.
  • Middleman Dominance: Field traders dictate farmgate rates, capturing the lion's share of retail profit.
  • Language & Literacy Barriers: Complex agritech software is built in academic English with steep learning curves.

The FarmIn Mission

Provide every rice farmer with three definitive answers: What will rice sell for?, When is the best time to sell?, and What is my true profit?

AI Forecasting & Sell Alerts

SARIMA Price Forecast

6-Month Horizon

Trained on 60 monthly PSA observations (2021–2025). The seasonal SARIMA (2,1,2)(0,0,2)[12] model achieves an optimal AIC of 639.79, projecting future farmgate movements.

Algorithmic Sell/Hold Alerts

Peak Detection

Scans future price peaks and triggers a clear SELL NOW or HOLD badge with estimated extra income (e.g. +₱6,000 per 5 MT yield).

Supply & Demand Balance

PSA Volume

Quarterly palay volume synthesis from PSA data tracks regional supply levels, helping farmers understand market scarcity.

Decision Tools & Inclusivity

Smart Agricultural Calculators

Three purpose-built field calculators tailor estimates to exact plot dimensions:

  • Profit Calculator: Pairs predicted price with yield and official production costs to show actual net margins.
  • Seed Calculator: Computes bag requirements for direct seeding vs. transplanting methods.
  • Fertilizer Calculator: Computes N-P-K splits based on farm size and target yield.

Weather-Aware Planting Calendar

Integrates real-time OpenWeatherMap data for Laoag City with agronomic guidelines across 4 distinct rice growth stages.

100% English + Ilokano Bilingual

Every single screen, financial figure, and advisory warning is natively translated to Ilokano to eliminate linguistic exclusion.
