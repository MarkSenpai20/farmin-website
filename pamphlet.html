🌾 The Smallholder Challenge

Closing the Agricultural Asymmetry Gap

Rice farmers in Ilocos Norte face severe price volatility. During harvest gluts, lack of predictive market visibility forces farmers into panic selling at depressed field prices dictated by traders.

  • Price Information Gap: Farmers sell upon harvest without knowing if prices will surge within weeks.
  • Middleman Leverage: Traders capitalize on urgency, capturing the majority of retail markups.
  • Complex Software: Existing tools are in academic English with steep learning barriers.

Three Concrete Answers Delivered:

1. What will rice sell for? (SARIMA 6-month farmgate trajectory)

2. When should I sell? (SELL NOW vs. HOLD harvest recommendation)

3. What will I actually earn? (Net profit accounting for input costs)

Algorithmic Sell/Hold Alerts

Peak Detection

Scans the 6-month prediction horizon. When a price peak is detected (e.g. November peak at ₱21.40 vs August ₱20.20), FarmIn advises holding, projecting an estimated extra income of +₱6,000 per 5,000 kg harvest.

Regional Supply-Demand Equilibrium

Incorporates PSA quarterly palay production volume for Ilocos Norte, providing an exponential-smoothing indicator of whether the province is in surplus or deficit.
