The Research Challenge

Empowering Smallholders Against Market Uncertainty

Rice farming in Ilocos Norte has long suffered from asymmetric price information. Smallholder farmers often sell in panic during harvest gluts, leaving substantial profit on the table.

📉
The Problem

Volatile Farmgate Prices

Traders leverage sudden seasonal price drops. Farmers lack predictive foresight and sell immediately at lower harvest rates rather than timing peak market conditions.

🧠
Our Innovation

Seasonal Time-Series AI

By integrating monthly PSA historical data with automated SARIMA modeling, FarmIn forecasts farmgate price trends 6 months ahead with measurable statistical confidence.

🗣️
Real Accessibility

Zero-Friction Inclusivity

Native Ilokano translations, guest access mode without required email registration, and full offline caching ensure even remote barangays benefit.

Key Capabilities

Designed Specifically for the Field

Every feature translates dense agricultural datasets into actionable decisions a farmer can trust.

📈
Module 1

6-Month Farmgate Forecast

Trained on 60+ months of official PSA data. Distinguishes farmgate field rates from retail supermarket prices to prevent misleading margins.

🔔
Module 2

Smart Sell Alerts

Automated peak-month detection. Plain-language recommendation: SELL NOW or HOLD, paired with estimated profit gains.

🧮
Module 3

Financial & Field Calculators

Dynamic calculations for Net Profit, Seed requirements (direct seeding vs transplanting), and Nitrogen-Phosphorus-Potassium fertilizer bags.

📅
Module 4

Weather-Aware Calendar

Synchronized with live Laoag OpenWeatherMap conditions. Displays phase-by-phase agronomic tips and warnings in English and Ilokano.

⚖️
Module 5

Regional Supply-Demand

Exponential-smoothing model estimating palay surpluses and deficits across Ilocos Norte, contextualizing why prices fluctuate.

💾
Module 6

Offline Write-Through Cache

Zero-network resilience. The app automatically caches previous predictions and inputs, working flawlessly even in deep rural fields.

SARIMA Farmgate Price Trajectory

The app projects regional farmgate prices (dry palay) for the upcoming 6 months using monthly seasonal parameters (period = 12).

  • Trained on PSA historical farmgate series (Jan 2021 – Dec 2025)
  • Dual price tracking: Farmgate field prices separated from DA Retail Bantay Presyo
  • Serverless static delivery ensures instantaneous load with 0 cold start

Algorithmic Sell/Hold Advisor

Takes the stress out of market timing. Instead of raw charts, FarmIn provides a single, unambiguous recommendation with financial rationale.

  • Peak price detection across the active forecast window
  • Quantifies exact estimated extra income gained by holding vs selling early
  • Adapts to user harvest month selection
RECOMMENDATION: HOLD Peak: November

"Prices are expected to rise by ₱1.20/kg between now and November. If you hold dry harvest for 6 weeks, your projected bonus revenue is ₱6,000 per 5,000 kg."

* Accounts for standard storage conditions and moisture levels.

Interactive Net Profit Estimator

Farmers can enter their farm size and expected yield. FarmIn pairs this with the predicted farmgate price and baseline regional input costs.

  • Formula: (Forecast Price × Yield in kg) − Total Input Expenses
  • Saved directly to farmer's cloud record or local offline ledger

System Design & Engineering

Serverless, Zero-Cost Forecasting Pipeline

Built for zero downtime and instant response without burdening researchers with recurring server bills.

Step 1

Data Aggregation

Pulls 60-month PSA OpenSTAT farmgate series + daily Department of Agriculture Bantay Presyo market scrapes.

Step 2

Automated Training

Scheduled GitHub Actions pipeline fits auto_arima in memory with seasonal parameters (SARIMA AIC: 639.8).

Step 3

Static CDN Delivery

Generates pre-rendered JSON payloads to GitHub CDN. Eliminates cold-start server lag and 100% of server hosting costs.

Step 4

Mobile Client & Sync

Flutter app caches data locally for full offline resilience, while Supabase handles PostgreSQL storage & Row-Level Security.
